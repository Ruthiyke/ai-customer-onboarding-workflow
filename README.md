# AI-Assisted Customer Onboarding & Support Workflow (Learning Project)

**Created by:** Ruth Ola Ibekwe
**Project type:** Self-directed learning prototype / workflow design
**Tools:** ChatGPT, Google Sheets or Excel, HubSpot (foundational learning)

## Project overview

This project demonstrates how AI can support a consistent customer onboarding process for a small SaaS team. It is a learning prototype and workflow design, not a production integration.

## Problem

New customers often receive inconsistent onboarding. Important details can be missed, follow-ups may be late, and support teams may spend time rewriting the same messages.

## Proposed workflow

1. Capture customer name, company, use case, onboarding date, owner, status, and next step in a simple tracker.
2. Use an AI assistant to draft a personalized welcome message based on the customer's stated use case.
3. Generate a short onboarding checklist with milestones: kickoff, setup, first workflow completed, team training, and value check-in.
4. Use the tracker to flag overdue milestones and customers who have not reached their first value milestone.
5. Draft a follow-up message or support response for human review.
6. Review common questions and feedback weekly to identify improvements to onboarding documentation.

## Example AI prompt

> You are a customer success associate for a workflow automation SaaS product. Based only on the customer details below, draft a warm welcome email, a 5-step onboarding checklist, and one clarifying question. Do not invent product capabilities or customer facts. Keep the language clear and practical.
>
> Customer company: [Company]
> Team: [Team]
> Main recurring process to improve: [Process]
> Desired outcome: [Outcome]
> Current onboarding stage: [Stage]

## Example tracker fields

- Customer / company
- Main use case
- Desired outcome
- Onboarding owner
- Onboarding stage
- First-value milestone
- Next action
- Due date
- Risk / blocker
- Feedback / questions

## Success measures

- Time to first value
- Onboarding milestone completion rate
- Overdue follow-ups
- Common support questions
- Customer feedback and satisfaction

## Responsible-use notes

- A human reviews all AI-generated customer messages before sending.
- Do not enter passwords, payment information, confidential business data, or sensitive personal information into a public AI tool.
- AI must not invent product features, commitments, or customer facts.
- This prototype describes a workflow; it does not claim to be a live integration or deployed application.

## What I learned / next steps

This project connects AI-assisted drafting with a structured onboarding process. Next, I would test the checklist with sample data, refine the prompts, and explore whether parts of the workflow could be automated through a CRM or no-code tool.

### 1. Welcome email

Hello Zenith Heights Compliance Team,

Welcome! We're delighted to have you on board and look forward to supporting your team as you work to improve client compliance tracking.

We understand that reducing missed audit deadlines is an important priority for your team. During onboarding, we'll work together to understand your current process, identify areas for improvement, and establish a practical approach to managing your recurring compliance activities.

As we begin, we'd love to learn more about how your team currently tracks client compliance requirements and manages upcoming audit deadlines.

Warm regards,
Customer Success Team

### 2. Five-step onboarding checklist

| Step | Action | Expected outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Understand the current process | Discuss how the team tracks requirements, monitors deadlines and follows up on outstanding items. | A clear understanding of the existing process and its challenges. |
| 2. Identify key requirements | Identify the compliance activities, audit deadlines and client information the team needs to track. | An agreed list of essential tracking requirements. |
| 3. Map the workflow | Document the steps from identifying requirements to completing audit-related activities. | A structured view of the process and potential gaps. |
| 4. Establish the tracking approach | Agree how activities, responsibilities and deadlines will be recorded and monitored. | An agreed approach that reduces the risk of missed deadlines. |
| 5. Review and confirm readiness | Review the workflow with the team and agree the next steps. | Alignment on the onboarding plan. |

**How to measure progress:** missed audit deadlines, on-time completion rate, and outstanding compliance items, compared against a baseline.

### 3. Clarifying question

How does your Compliance team currently track client compliance requirements and audit deadlines, for example with spreadsheets, a dedicated system, or a manual process?
Why this matters; understanding the current approach will help us identify the main gaps, tailor the onboarding process to your team's need and determine the most practical way to reduce missed audit deadlines.
