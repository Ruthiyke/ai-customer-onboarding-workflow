AI-Assisted Customer Onboarding & Support Workflow (Learning Project)

Created by: Ruth Ola Ibekwe
Project type: Self-directed learning prototype / workflow design
Tools: ChatGPT, Google Sheets or Excel, HubSpot (foundational learning)

Project overview

This project demonstrates how AI can support a consistent customer onboarding process for a small SaaS team. It is a learning prototype and workflow design, not a production integration.

Problem

New customers often receive inconsistent onboarding. Important details can be missed, follow-ups may be late, and support teams may spend time rewriting the same messages.

Proposed workflow

Capture customer name, company, use case, onboarding date, owner, status, and next step in a simple tracker.

Use an AI assistant to draft a personalized welcome message based on the customer's stated use case.

Generate a short onboarding checklist with milestones: kickoff, setup, first workflow completed, team training, and value check-in.

Use the tracker to flag overdue milestones and customers who have not reached their first value milestone.

Draft a follow-up message or support response for human review.

Review common questions and feedback weekly to identify improvements to onboarding documentation.

Example AI prompt

You are a customer success associate for a workflow automation SaaS product. Based only on the customer details below, draft a warm welcome email, a 5-step onboarding checklist, and one clarifying question. Do not invent product capabilities or customer facts. Keep the language clear and practical.

Customer company: [Company]
Team: [Team]
Main recurring process to improve: [Process]
Desired outcome: [Outcome]

Responsible-use notes

A human reviews all AI-generated customer messages before sending.

Do not enter passwords, payment information, confidential business data, or sensitive personal information into a public AI tool.

AI must not invent product features, commitments, or customer facts.

This prototype describes a workflow; it does not claim to be a live integration or deployed application.

What I learned / next steps

This project connects AI-assisted drafting with a structured onboarding process. Next, I would test the checklist with sample data, refine the prompts, and explore whether parts of the workflow could be automated through a CRM or no-code tool.
